## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the sensory intimacy, geological heritage, and quiet luxury of contemporary Galician haute cuisine. It positions digital interaction not as transactional software, but as an editorial prelude to an immersive culinary experience.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Atmosphere
- **Architectural & Rooted:** Inspired by the ancient Roman granite walls of Lugo and the verdant inland river basins of Galicia, the UI reflects grounded permanence balanced with contemporary restraint.
- **Editorial Sophistication:** Evoking the feel of a printed art monograph or independent culinary publication—prioritizing expansive negative space, deliberate typography pacing, and unhurried visual storytelling.
- **Sensory Warmth:** Distancing itself from cold, clinical fine-dining tropes through tactile earthen hues, warm fiber-like backgrounds, and soft, natural tonal layering.

### Design Movement
**Warm Editorial Minimalism with Tactile Restraint.** The system rejects loud decorative gradients, bouncy synthetic animations, and cookie-cutter SaaS layouts. Instead, it relies on structured baseline rhythms, asymmetric compositional balance, delicate rules, and high-contrast typographic pairings.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from raw culinary material, stone, and flora.

- **Primary (`#171716` - Deep Charcoal):** Grounded, high-contrast ink color for titles, body copy, and definitive interactive elements. Replaces harsh synthetic pure black with organic density.
- **Secondary (`#3F4939` - Dark Olive Green):** Echoing the native Galician forest canopy and herbal infusions; applied to primary interactive surfaces, badges, and contextual highlights.
- **Accent (`#A56B4F` - Terracotta Copper):** Evoking traditional fired clay cookware, embers, and macerated wines; used selectively for alerts, availability status, active indicators, and subtle typographic accents.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F3EEE5` - Warm Ivory):** The primary page canvas. A warm, uncoated-paper tone that softens digital glare and honors traditional print mediums.
- **Supporting Tones:**
  - `Secondary Background` (`#E8E0D4` - Soft Cream): Used for structural panels, subtle tonal distinctions, and contained reservation drawers.
  - `Surface High-Tone` (`#FAF9F6` - Clean Alabaster White): Used sparingly for elevated modal containers and focused interactive forms.
  - `Subtle Divider` (`rgba(23, 23, 22, 0.08)`): For hairline borders, mimicking bookbinding rules.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes a dialogue between historical presence and modern culinary craft.

- **Display & Headings (EB Garamond):** Used for dish titles, tasting menu acts, chapter introductions, and seasonal narratives. Features classic proportional elegance, lyrical italic forms, and warm humanist serifs.
- **Body & Functional UI (Manrope):** A clean grotesque sans-serif with geometric foundations and open apertures that balances the romanticism of Garamond with impeccable functional legibility.
- **Rules of Engagement:**
  - `label-caps` is always rendered in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.12em`) to introduce sections, origins of ingredients, or pairing categories.
  - `body-editorial` provides an optional italicized or regular Garamond variant for tasting menu pacing, chef manifestos, and tasting notes.
  - Never set headings in heavy weights; the elegance of the brand relies on the delicate weight distribution (`400`/`500`) of classical Roman proportions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around asymmetric editorial pacing, inspired by architectural floor plans and high-end monographs rather than standard multi-column web templates.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column variable grid with 64px (`margin`) outer margins and 24px (`gutter`) gutters. Sections often utilize 5/7 or 4/8 column pairings to create off-center, editorial tensions.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 32px (`margin-tablet`) margins and 20px gutters.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout with 20px (`margin-mobile`) margins and 16px (`gutter-mobile`) gutters.

### Editorial Composition Rules
- **Intentional Asymmetry:** Gastronomic courses and wine selections are never placed in uniform auto-grids. They flow sequentially with varying offsets, paired imagery, and poetic descriptions.
- **Generous Vertical Breathing Room:** Section transitions utilize deep vertical padding (`4rem` to `7rem`), ensuring each dish or narrative concept is absorbed without sensory competition.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately eschews synthetic drop shadows and floating glass panels. Depth is created through architectural surface hierarchy and hairline tactile borders.

- **Tonal Layers:**
  - Level 0 (Base Canvas): Warm Ivory (`#F3EEE5`) for general backgrounds.
  - Level 1 (Structural Insets): Soft Cream (`#E8E0D4`) for distinct menu groupings, reservation date pickers, or chef profiles.
  - Level 2 (Foreground Floating Layers): Clean White (`#FAF9F6`) used strictly for dynamic modals, checkout sheets, and reservation confirmation dialogs.
- **Hairline Framing:** In place of shadows, surfaces are demarcated with `1px` borders colored in `rgba(23, 23, 22, 0.09)`.
- **Atmospheric Veil:** When modal overlays appear, the backdrop is veiled with `#171716` at 40% opacity with no blur, maintaining crisp editorial geometry.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly architectural (`roundedness: 0`), reflecting the carved stone facades and ancient Roman walls of Lugo.

- **Sharp Corners (0px):** Buttons, images, modal dialogs, cards, input frames, and category tabs all maintain unrounded, 90-degree corners.
- **Inner Framing:** Imagery is often inset within structural borders or framed with asymmetric margins to simulate gallery prints mounted on museum matting.
- **Micro-Pill Exceptions:** Tiny metadata badges (e.g., vintage year, course number) may utilize a subtle 1px border with crisp right-angles, never rounded pill shapes.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary CTA:** Deep Charcoal (`#171716`) background, Clean White (`#FAF9F6`) text, sharp corners, padded at `14px 28px`. Hover state shifts seamlessly to Dark Olive (`#3F4939`) with a 200ms ease.
- **Secondary CTA:** Transparent background, 1px solid border in `#171716`, deep charcoal text. Hover fills the interior with `#E8E0D4`.
- **Text Link CTA:** All-caps Manrope (`label-caps`), underlined with a hairline rule 4px below the text baseline. Hover transitions the underline color to Terracotta Copper (`#A56B4F`).

### Form Fields & Inputs (Reservations)
- **Input Architecture:** Minimalist horizontal baseline field or boxed crisp-edged input on `#FAF9F6` with a 1px border (`rgba(23, 23, 22, 0.15)`).
- **Active / Focus:** Border transitions to Terracotta Copper (`#A56B4F`) with no outer glow or drop shadow.
- **Labels:** Set in `label-caps`, pinned closely above the field.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** Crisp 16px squares with 1px border. Checked state fills with `#3F4939` with an ivory check mark.
- **Radio Options (Tasting vs. A La Carte):** Minimal circular ring with a solid charcoal dot on selection, paired with descriptive editorial copy.

### Gastronomic Menu Items (Replaces Generic Cards)
- **Structure:** Horizontal or staggered editorial rows separated by subtle 1px rules (`rgba(23, 23, 22, 0.08)`).
- **Anatomy:** 
  1. Course number / index in `label-caps` (`#A56B4F`).
  2. Dish name in `headline-sm` (`#171716`).
  3. Regional provenance and botanical ingredients in `body-sm` (`rgba(23, 23, 22, 0.7)`).
  4. Subtle price or pairing pairing note aligned to the right.

### Sommelier & Cellar Drawer
- Slide-in lateral drawer utilizing `#FAF9F6` background, allowing guests to explore vintage pairings, producers, and terroir notes with uninterrupted focus.
